# Company / Recruiter Components (`src/components/company`)

## Overview
Provides the recruiter-facing workspace UI layer, layout framing, URL-synced dashboard tabs, challenge generation controls, cross-challenge candidate review views, and the three-pane individual submission review workspace. Server-compatible primitives live in `recruiter-shell.tsx`, `ui.tsx`, `score-rec-styles.ts`, and `submission-picks.ts`, while interactive client flows (`RecruiterTabProvider`/`RecruiterTabNav`/`RecruiterTabPanel`, `RecruiterUnifiedDashboardClient`, `RecruiterDashboardChallengesTab`, `RecruiterCandidatesReview`, `ReviewWorkspace`, `GenerateChallengeBtn`, `SubmissionsClickableRow`, and `UnifiedDashboardBanner`) manage tab state, server-action fetching, challenge generation, polling, and review navigation. Most public exports are aggregated in `index.ts` (excluding `recruiter-tabs.tsx`, `recruiter-dashboard-challenges-tab.tsx`, `score-rec-styles.ts`, and the prop types `RecruiterUnifiedDashboardClientProps` and `RecruiterCandidatesReviewProps`).

## Invariants
- **Tab context & URL canonicalization**: `useRecruiterTab()` throws `Error('useRecruiterTab must be used within RecruiterTabProvider')` outside `RecruiterTabProvider` (`RecruiterDashboardChallengesTab` depends on this context) `[recruiter-tabs.tsx:useRecruiterTab]`, `[recruiter-dashboard-challenges-tab.tsx:RecruiterDashboardChallengesTab]`. `parseTab` defaults unknown/missing `?tab=` values to `'pipeline'`; `setTab('pipeline')` removes `tab` from `/dashboard` query params while preserving all other params `[recruiter-tabs.tsx:parseTab]`, `[recruiter-tabs.tsx:RecruiterTabProvider]`. `RecruiterTabNav` enforces roving `tabIndex` (`0`/`-1`), cyclic keyboard navigation (`ArrowRight`/`ArrowLeft`/`Home`/`End`) over `['pipeline', 'challenges', 'review']`, and ARIA IDs `recruiter-tab-${id}` ↔ `recruiter-panel-${id}`; inactive `RecruiterTabPanel`s return `null` `[recruiter-tabs.tsx:RecruiterTabNav]`, `[recruiter-tabs.tsx:RecruiterTabPanel]`.
- **Unified dashboard composition**: `RecruiterUnifiedDashboardClient` wraps its body in `RecruiterTabProvider`, keys `<JobPostList key={statJobPosts} />` on `statJobPosts` (falling back to `EmptyState` when `statJobPosts === 0`), defaults the mobile uploader collapsed (`max-md:hidden`), expects `#dashboard-job-post-uploader` in the `pipeline` panel for smooth scrolling, and wires empty review CTA to `setTab('challenges')` `[recruiter-unified-dashboard-client.tsx:RecruiterUnifiedDashboardClient]`, `[recruiter-unified-dashboard-client.tsx:RecruiterUnifiedDashboardInner]`.
- **Challenge generation state machine**: `GenerateChallengeBtn` short-circuits on `'error' in result` (leaving `created === false` and skipping `onChallengeCreated`/`router.refresh()`), renders mutually exclusive states for `alreadyGenerated && !created` (disabled), `created` (confirmation banner), or active button disabled while `isPending` `[generate-challenge-btn.tsx:GenerateChallengeBtn]`, `[generate-challenge-btn.tsx:runCreate]`.
- **Score & recommendation thresholds**: `scoreHex`, `scoreClass`, and `scoreBadgeTier` share exact boundaries: high `>= 80`, mid `>= 60`, low `< 60`; `Recommendation` is strictly `'recommend' | 'consider' | 'pass'` (validated by `isValidRec` in `review-workspace.tsx` and narrowed in `submissions-table-row.tsx`) `[score-rec-styles.ts:scoreHex]`, `[ui.tsx:ScoreBadge]`, `[review-workspace.tsx:isValidRec]`. `EmptyState` prioritizes `href` (`<Link>`) over `onCtaClick` (`<button>`) `[ui.tsx:EmptyState]`.
- **Top-picks selection**: `pickSubmissionTopRows` (default `max = 3`) filters `status === 'scored' && score != null`, prioritizes `recommendation === 'recommend'` before descending score backfill, and deduplicates by `id` `[submission-picks.ts:pickSubmissionTopRows]`. `topPickRows` in `recruiter-candidates-review.tsx` (default `max = 4`) prioritizes `recommendation === 'recommend' || score >= 80` before descending score backfill `[recruiter-candidates-review.tsx:topPickRows]`.
- **Challenges tab guards**: `RecruiterDashboardChallengesTab` discriminates errors via `'error' in res`, enforces strict branch precedence (standalone error → empty state with `setTab('pipeline')` → loading skeleton → controls + table/cards), filters on `'all' | 'active' | 'draft' | 'scored'` (`topScore != null`), sorts on `'recent' | 'submissions' | 'score'`, and resolves engineering discipline labels via `tDash.has(...)` fallback `[recruiter-dashboard-challenges-tab.tsx:RecruiterDashboardChallengesTab]`, `[recruiter-dashboard-challenges-tab.tsx:engineeringDisciplineLabel]`.
- **Candidates review pagination & filtering**: `RecruiterCandidatesReview` paginates at `PAGE_SIZE = 20`, resets `page` to `1` on filter (`'all' | 'recommend' | 'consider' | 'pass'`) or sort (`'score' | 'recent'`) change, clamps `safePage` to `[1, totalPages]`, and renders pagination only when `filtered.length > 20` `[recruiter-candidates-review.tsx:RecruiterCandidatesReview]`.
- **Submission row & review workspace states**:
  - `SubmissionsClickableRow` renders a `<tr>` (`role='link'`, `tabIndex={0}`), shows shimmer cells when `status === 'submitted' || status === 'scoring'`, renders a `scoreClass`-styled `<span>` plus `RecPill` (and `StatusBadge`) when scored, and activates on click or `'Enter'`/`' '` `[submissions-table-row.tsx:SubmissionsClickableRow]`.
  - `ReviewWorkspace` sorts `allSubmissions` descending by `score ?? -1`, polls strictly while `status === 'submitted' || status === 'scoring'`, renders 4 mutually exclusive center states (`scoring`, `'failed'`, `'scored'`, other/waiting), renders a pulsing loading skeleton in `EvidencePanel` when `status === 'submitted' || status === 'scoring'` and returns `null` for other non-scored states or when `score == null`, and maintains a single-open `InterviewGuideAccordion` defaulting to index `0` `[review-workspace.tsx:ReviewWorkspace]`, `[review-workspace.tsx:EvidencePanel]`, `[review-workspace.tsx:InterviewGuideAccordion]`.
- **Banner tab constraint**: `UnifiedDashboardBanner` restricts `tab` to `'challenges' | 'review'` mapped to `/dashboard?tab=challenges` or `/dashboard?tab=review` `[unified-dashboard-banner.tsx:UnifiedDashboardBanner]`.

## Side Effects
- **Server actions**:
  - `createChallenge(jobPostId)` (`@/app/actions/challenge`) in `GenerateChallengeBtn`, followed by `sonner` error/success toasts, `onChallengeCreated`, and `router.refresh()` / `router.push('/company/challenges/${challengeId}')` `[generate-challenge-btn.tsx:runCreate]`.
  - `listRecruiterChallengesDashboard()` (`@/app/actions/challenge`) on mount in `RecruiterDashboardChallengesTab` `[recruiter-dashboard-challenges-tab.tsx:load]`.
  - `listAllSubmissions()` (`@/app/actions/challenge`) on mount in `RecruiterCandidatesReview` `[recruiter-candidates-review.tsx:RecruiterCandidatesReview]`.
- **Router navigation & polling**:
  - `router.replace('/dashboard' | '/dashboard?...')` on tab switch `[recruiter-tabs.tsx:RecruiterTabProvider]`.
  - `router.push('/company/challenges/${challengeId}/submissions/${id}')` in `SubmissionsClickableRow`, `RecruiterCandidatesReview`, and `ReviewWorkspace`'s `CandidateRow` `[submissions-table-row.tsx:SubmissionsClickableRow]`, `[recruiter-candidates-review.tsx:RecruiterCandidatesReview]`, `[review-workspace.tsx:CandidateRow]`.
  - `window.setInterval(() => router.refresh(), 5000)` in `ReviewWorkspace` while `scoring` is `true` (cleared on cleanup/status change) `[review-workspace.tsx:ReviewWorkspace]`.
- **DOM & browser APIs**:
  - Imperative tab `.focus()` on keyboard navigation `[recruiter-tabs.tsx:RecruiterTabNav]`.
  - `requestAnimationFrame` + `document.getElementById('dashboard-job-post-uploader')?.scrollIntoView({ behavior: 'smooth', block: 'start' })` `[recruiter-unified-dashboard-client.tsx:RecruiterUnifiedDashboardInner]`.
  - `window.print()` from Print buttons in `InterviewGuideAccordion` and `EvidencePanel` `[review-workspace.tsx:InterviewGuideAccordion]`, `[review-workspace.tsx:EvidencePanel]`.
- **Pure / declarative modules**: `index.ts`, `recruiter-shell.tsx`, `score-rec-styles.ts`, `submission-picks.ts`, `ui.tsx`, and `unified-dashboard-banner.tsx` perform no imperative side effects beyond declarative `<Link>` navigation or caller callbacks.
